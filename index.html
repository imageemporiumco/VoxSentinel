Scientific Detection Methodology

Autocorrelation Prosodic Analysis (F0 Tracking)

Human conversational speech carries natural vocal folds modulation (prosodic micro-variations). We track the fundamental frequency (F0) in 30ms sliding windows. Cloned voices generated from Text-To-Speech (TTS) models often exhibit flat prosodic pitch ranges, or contain abrupt frequency steps representing pitch tracking faults in vocoder synthesis models.

Brickwall Spectral Boundary limits

Deep learning voice generators frequently synthesize audio at constrained sampling rates (e.g. 16,000 Hz or 24,000 Hz) due to memory requirements. This manifests as a sudden lack of high frequency spectral components (brickwall filter cutoff at 8kHz or 12kHz). The platform monitors the **Spectral Rolloff Boundary** (frequency containing 85% of total signal energy) to find these synthesis sampling filters.

Spectral Centroid and Resonances

We calculate the **Spectral Centroid** to represent the center of spectral weight. Vocoder models can yield phase irregularities that compress formant resonances, creating abnormally flat or repetitive spectral centroid pathways across vocalized vowels.

Forensic Limitations & Legal Disclaimers

CRITICAL CYBERSECURITY PRINCIPLE: Detection is NOT Proof.
VoiceGuard is an investigative and educational tool, not an absolute proof provider. Synthesized voice models continuously evolve, and compression or codec filtering can mask or create artifacts. Verdicts should be aggregated as diagnostic evidence within broader security investigations.

Known Analysis Constraints

  • Codec Compression: Forwarded voice clips (e.g. WhatsApp, Telegram) utilize low-bitrate codecs (Opus/AAC) which strip high frequencies, occasionally producing features similar to synthesis cutoffs.
  • Acoustic Background Noise: Strong background hums or transient noise lower the Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), masking vocoder signatures.
  • Utterance Duration: Clips under 1.5s do not contain enough speech structures for reliable pitch autocorrelation metrics, resulting in inconclusive statuses.

Sensitive Data & Privacy Declarations

We recognize voice recordings as highly sensitive identifiers. The VoiceGuard platform handles audio files in-memory on the client browser. No audio recordings are permanently stored on backend filesystems. Forensic reports only cache extracted numerical metrics, metadata, and final scores to compile local history registers.
